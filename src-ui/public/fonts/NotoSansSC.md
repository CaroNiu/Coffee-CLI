Noto Sans SC variable font (weights 100–900), converted losslessly from TTF
to WOFF2 with fontTools. Used by the sidebar and workspace UI; native macOS CJK fonts take
precedence. All glyphs are retained for user-provided project/session names.

Upstream: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanssc
